# About WallBang

**Last updated:** August 28, 2026

WallBang is a Counter-Strike 2 community platform focused on providing high-quality retake servers for competitive players.

We operate community gaming servers designed to provide fast, reliable, and competitive retake gameplay. WallBang is **India first**, with low-latency CS2 retake servers as the initial priority.

## What we offer

WallBang provides **online gaming / digital gaming services** through Counter-Strike 2 community retake servers. Players can join public retake servers and use platform features such as Steam login, player profiles, and loadouts as they roll out on the roadmap.

WallBang also offers optional **prepaid VIP memberships** for WallBang's Counter-Strike 2 community servers. VIP provides additional server access, community, and cosmetic in-server features such as chat tags, priority queue, and cosmetic loadout options. VIP does not provide gambling, betting, monetary rewards, prize-based gaming, or competitive advantages that change core gameplay.

## Our commitment

WallBang is built for serious Counter-Strike 2 players. We do not sell CS2 skins, Steam items, or operate a skin marketplace. Cosmetic features available through VIP are in-server only and are part of the WallBang service — not transferable game items.
