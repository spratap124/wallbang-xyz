# Cancellation Policy

**Effective Date:** September 2, 2026

WallBang sells prepaid, fixed-duration Hosted Server Access. Access is paid once for the selected term and is not automatically renewed.

## Cancellation Before Activation

A customer may request cancellation of a purchase within 7 days of purchase, provided the purchased hosted server access has not been activated or used.

Eligible cancellation requests will be handled according to the [Refund Policy](https://wallbang.xyz/refund).

## Cancellation After Activation

Once hosted server access has been activated or used, cancellation does not automatically result in a refund.

Customers may contact WallBang if they experience a technical issue preventing them from receiving the purchased server access privileges.

## Automatic Renewal

WallBang does not automatically renew hosted server access and does not automatically charge customers again.

A new purchase is required to continue access after the current term ends.

## Expiry

Hosted server access automatically ends at the end of the purchased duration.

After expiry, reserved access and related privileges are removed from the customer's account.
