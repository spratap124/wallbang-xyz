# Cancellation Policy

**Effective Date:** August 31, 2026

WallBang provides prepaid digital gaming services.

## Cancellation Before Activation

A customer may request cancellation of a purchase within 7 days of purchase, provided the purchased VIP service has not been activated or used.

Eligible cancellation requests will be handled according to the [Refund Policy](https://wallbang.xyz/refund).

## Cancellation After Activation

Once VIP access has been activated or used, cancellation of the service does not automatically result in a refund.

Customers may contact WallBang if they experience a technical issue preventing them from receiving the purchased service.

## Automatic Renewal

WallBang does not provide automatic subscription renewal.

Customers are never automatically charged for renewal. A new purchase is required to renew VIP access.

## Expiry

VIP access automatically expires at the end of the purchased duration.

After expiry, VIP privileges are removed from the customer's account.

Cancellation requests can be submitted to:

**Email:** [admin@wallbang.xyz](mailto:admin@wallbang.xyz)
