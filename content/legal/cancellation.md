# Cancellation Policy

**Effective Date:** September 2, 2026

WallBang sells prepaid, fixed-duration VIP memberships. VIP is paid once for the selected term and is not automatically renewed.

## Cancellation Before Activation

A customer may request cancellation of a purchase within 7 days of purchase, provided the purchased VIP membership has not been activated or used.

Eligible cancellation requests will be handled according to the [Refund Policy](https://wallbang.xyz/refund).

## Cancellation After Activation

Once VIP has been activated or used, cancellation does not automatically result in a refund.

Customers may contact WallBang if they experience a technical issue preventing them from receiving the purchased membership privileges.

## Automatic Renewal

WallBang does not automatically renew VIP and does not automatically charge customers again.

A new purchase is required to continue VIP after the current term ends.

## Expiry

VIP automatically ends at the end of the purchased duration.

After expiry, VIP privileges are removed from the customer's account.
